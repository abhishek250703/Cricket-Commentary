Most iconic and best cricket commentary lines in history

1. Dhoni finishes off in style!

2011 ICC Cricket World Cup final

🎙 Commentator: Ravi Shastri

Dhoni finishes off in style! was Ravi Shastri iconic commentary line when MS Dhoni hit a massive straight six off Nuwan Kulasekara to win the 2011 ICC Cricket World Cup final at the Wankhede Stadium, Mumbai, on 2 April 2011. Chasing 275 against Sri Lanka, Dhoni promoted himself up the order and played a calm, match-winning innings of 91 off 79 balls, guiding India home and ending a 28-year wait for the World Cup. The moment became immortal because the Indian captain sealed the title with a six in front of a roaring home crowd, turning that single line into one of the most memorable pieces of commentary in cricket history.

2. Remember the name, Carlos Brathwaite!

2016 ICC T20 World Cup Final, West Indies vs England

🎙 Commentator: Ian Bishop

Remember the name, Carlos Brathwaite! is the iconic commentary line delivered by Ian Bishop during the 2016 ICC T20 World Cup final. It came after Carlos Brathwaite smashed four consecutive sixes off Ben Stokes in the final over at Eden Gardens, Kolkata, to pull off an unbelievable win for West Indies against England. With 19 needed off the last over, Brathwaite fearless hitting stunned the cricketing world, and Bishop emotional line instantly captured the magnitude of the moment, turning Brathwaite from a relatively unknown player into a global cricketing hero.

3. England have won the World Cup by the barest of margins!

2019 ICC Cricket World Cup Final, England vs New Zealand at Lords

🎙 Commentator: Ian Bishop

The 2019 ICC Cricket World Cup final produced some unforgettable commentary moments as England edged New Zealand in the most dramatic finish ever. Ian Smith captured history with, England have won the World Cup by the barest of margins! as the Super Over ended in chaos and disbelief. Commentators like Nasser Hussain and Michael Holding reflected the shock by calling it an unbelievable finish and what a game of cricket, what a final. Ian Bishop summed up the surreal nature of the match by saying, You could not make this up, while others agreed it was the closest final cricket would ever witness. Together, these lines perfectly echoed the tension, drama, and sheer madness of a World Cup final that will never be forgotten.
